Privacy Policy

Baby Diary (Nhật Ký Bé Yêu) · Last updated 13 October 2026 · Đọc bằng tiếng Việt

The short version
  • We store your baby's log so your devices and your family can see it.
  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics tools. We never sell your data.
  • Photos stay on your phone.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app.

Baby Diary helps parents track bottle feeds, pumping, their breast milk stash, and their baby's growth and milestones, and share this with family. This policy explains what data we keep, why we keep it, and what you can do about it. It also covers Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection.

1. Who we are

Baby Diary is made by App Works 247. If you have any questions about your data, contact us through our support page.

2. What we store

  • Your baby's information, as you enter it: name, date of birth, sex, and how many weeks of pregnancy at birth; bottle feeds, pumping sessions and milk bags; weight, length and head size; milestones and notes. This is health information about a child, so the law treats it as sensitive personal data. We take extra care with it.
  • Your account: a random user ID. If you choose to link Apple or Google, we also keep your email address (with Apple, this can be a hidden relay address) and the name you show to your family.
  • Your family: who is in it, their roles, their names in the app, and invite codes.

We do not store on our servers: photos (milestone and baby photos stay on your phone), and your settings such as language, units and reminders.

We do not: show ads, track you across other apps or websites, use analytics or advertising tools, or sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Why we store it

  • To save your baby's log and sync it between your phones and the family members you invite.
  • To show you useful things, worked out on your phone: when milk expires, which bag to use first, growth percentiles from WHO data, and reminders.
  • To let you keep your data when you change phones, if you link Apple or Google.

4. Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), on Google Cloud servers in Singapore (asia-southeast1). This means your data is stored outside Vietnam. Google handles the data for us under the Firebase data processing terms. All connections are encrypted.

Premium purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.

5. Who can see it

Only you and the people you invite to your family. What they can see and change depends on their role: owner, parent, caregiver or viewer. Security rules on the server block everyone else.

6. Your consent

When you start using the app and enter your baby's information, you agree, as the parent or guardian, that we can store and use this data for the reasons in section 3. You can take back your consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 8).

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account or leave your family. When you delete an item in the app, it is marked as deleted so your other devices can see the change.

8. Your rights

  • See and change your data: you can view, edit and delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings → Account → Delete account. If you are the only family member, all of the family's data is deleted for good. If not, you leave the family and its data stays with the other members.
  • Get a copy, limit how we use it, or complain: contact us through our support page. Please don't post personal details there: we will reply and tell you how to reach us privately, within the time the law requires.

9. Children

Baby Diary is made for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. Parents enter information about their own baby.

10. Health information

Baby Diary gives general information based on CDC and WHO guidelines. It is not medical advice. Always ask your doctor about your baby's health.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
